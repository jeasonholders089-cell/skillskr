Before using this project publicly, or shipping it commercially:

1. Replace everything under `public/assets/` with your own artwork, or confirm

2. Replace `assets/logo.png` / `assets/logo-dark.png` with your own mark.
3. Check the typeface licence (`assets/fonts/bricolage-*.woff2`).

Everything outside `public/assets/` (server, schema, auth, billing, scripts and
the pack generator) can be used as the basis of your own product.
