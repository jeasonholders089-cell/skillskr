Learn skillskr

A practical guide to browsing, buying and installing AI Agent Skills.

1. Browse the catalog

Open /skills to browse free and Pro Skills. Use the category filters for slides, web/HTML, images, video and research, or search by keyword.

2. Choose a plan or a free Skill

Free Skills can be downloaded after signing in. Pro Skills require an active subscription. Current plans are listed on the pricing page:

  • Pro Monthly — US$9.99/month
  • Pro Yearly — US$79/year
  • Founding Lifetime — US$169 one-time

3. Install a Skill

After checkout or download, you will receive a Skill pack containing a SKILL.md file and supporting templates or scripts. The general installation steps are:

  1. Sign in at groclawa.com.
  2. Open the Skill page and download the pack.
  3. Unzip it and place the Skill folder in your agent's local skills directory.
  4. Restart the agent or reload its Skill list.
  5. Ask the agent to use the Skill on your own material.

For a machine-readable installation note, see /install/agent.md.

Exact folder names differ between Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and other agents. Follow the installation instructions for your agent; the Skill pack itself is portable.
